# Indian in Germany

A SwiftUI companion app for Indians planning a move to Germany or building their life there. It brings practical journey checklists, university and scholarship resources, local communities, safety guidance, and useful services into one place.

## Features

- Personalize guidance by city, life situation, and journey stage.
- Follow practical arrival and visa preparation checklists.
- Explore German universities, DAAD course listings, and uni-assist application resources.
- Find scholarship and funding options, including DAAD programmes and the Deutschlandstipendium.
- Discover Indian associations, student groups, and official community directories.
- Browse housing safety guidance and Germany settlement resources.
- Access contextual partner and referral offers. Referral relationships are disclosed in the app; offers and eligibility are subject to provider terms.

## Requirements

- macOS with Xcode installed.
- An iOS simulator or compatible iPhone for running the app.

## Open and run

1. Clone this repository.
2. Open `Indian in Germany.xcodeproj` in Xcode.
3. Select the **Indian in Germany** scheme and an iOS simulator.
4. Build and run.

## Project structure

- `Indian in Germany/` — SwiftUI app source and asset catalogs.
- `Indian in Germany.xcodeproj/` — Xcode project.

## Data and external resources

The app links to third-party and official resources. Their availability, requirements, deadlines, and terms may change. Always confirm visa, university admissions, scholarship, financial, and service requirements with the responsible official authority or provider. The app’s general information is not legal, immigration, tax, or investment advice.

The app currently stores journey selections and checklist completion locally on the device using `AppStorage`; it does not provide cloud account synchronization.
